WHY PLA?

  • Thermoplastic nature enables heat-based application
  • Creates continuous, water-resistant surface layer
  • Compatible with industrial composting facilities

IMPROVISED SETUP

  • Replicated hot press using hot plate with manual pressure
  • Used available laboratory materials for release arrangement
  • Demonstrated resourcefulness in product development

PLA: CHALLENGES & LEARNINGS

Understanding the limitations of our initial approach

UNEVEN SPREADING

Limited temperature uniformity and pressure control resulted in incomplete contact between PLA and the rough bagasse surface.

Temperature Control

THERMAL MISMATCH

Difference in thermal behaviour between polymer and lignocellulosic substrate caused coating inconsistency.

Material Compatibility

KEY INSIGHT

Controlled hot-pressing or compression-moulding would significantly improve results with proper process control.

Equipment Needs

WHAT WE LEARNED

The experiment demonstrated the limitations of thermoplastic coating without appropriate processing equipment. It gave us practical insight into the processing requirements for thermoplastic polymers.

WHY SEAWEED?

  • Naturally abundant and renewable resource
  • Edible and fully biodegradable coating
  • Inherent antimicrobial properties

DRYING CONDITIONS

  • Ambient laboratory conditions used
  • No controlled drying equipment available
  • Standardized coating equipment not accessible

SEAWEED: CHALLENGES & LEARNINGS

Understanding adhesion and film formation requirements

POOR ADHESION & UNIFORMITY

The bagasse plates' rough, porous surface absorbed the coating solution into pores, preventing continuous film formation. The dried coating showed poor mechanical durability.

CONTRIBUTING FACTORS

  • No surface pre-treatment performed
  • Uncontrolled coating conditions
  • Solution concentration not optimized
  • Viscosity and immersion time issues
  • Substrate surface roughness not addressed

KEY TAKEAWAY

  • Simply depositing material onto porous substrate is insufficient
  • Interfacial adhesion is critical for functional coating
  • Film formation requires controlled conditions
  • Surface pre-treatment may be necessary
